About the Training & Placement Department

Anil Neerukonda Institute of Technology & Sciences (ANITS) has a dedicated Training & Placement Department led by experienced faculty coordinators. The department is committed to maximizing student placements in both software and core engineering sectors through continuous skill development and industry engagement.

ANITS provides comprehensive campus recruitment training covering Quantitative Aptitude, Logical Reasoning, Verbal Reasoning, English Communication, Group Discussions, and Interview Skills. Students undergo regular practice sessions conducted by experienced trainers.

Recruiters appreciate the excellent placement infrastructure, including modern computer laboratories, seminar halls, interview rooms, and well-equipped examination facilities.

Through dedicated efforts and strong industry collaborations, ANITS has consistently achieved excellent placement records and is recognized among the top private engineering colleges.

Our Department Focuses On

  • Identifying employment opportunities for fresh engineering graduates.
  • Building strong industry-academia partnerships.
  • Providing comprehensive campus recruitment training programs.
  • Organizing industry expert lectures, workshops, and seminars.
  • Promoting innovation and entrepreneurship development.
  • Facilitating internships, industrial visits, and technical collaborations.
